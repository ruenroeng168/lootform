# Hook Lab — audience clarity and genuine retention

## The first frame
Ask: Can a stranger identify the subject, context and promise without an explanation? The goal is relevance, not just sensory shock. No unrelated pre-roll logos when the platform calls for a fast start. For premium brand films, intentional atmosphere is legitimate; for long-form, the opening can be deliberate but must establish a reason to stay.

## Hook mechanisms across formats
1. **Direct buyer question** (review): "สิ่งที่ควรดูเวลาเลือกเครื่องรุ่นนี้คืออะไร?"
2. **Visible proof or honest demonstration** (demo/sales): actual product action and a verified claim or use-case.
3. **Relatable situation** (comedy): an unexpected professional or household conflict.
4. **Useful outcome** (tutorial): show the finished result, then the process to get it.
5. **Consequence / stakes** (documentary): a genuine moment framed fairly with context.
6. **Important insight** (interview): a quote that retains actual speaker meaning.
7. **Visual aspiration** (brand/cinematic): authentic place/object or evocative mood tied to the story.
8. **Specific comparison** (review/explainer): declared criteria, not fake "versus" drama.

## Choosing hooks
Use the 0–5 rubric in `13-hooks-by-objective.md`: clarity, relevance, curiosity/tension, credibility, fulfillment. Propose 3 mechanisms, not 3 synonyms. Keep a first-frame idea and credible payoff for each. Tests inform learning only when actual comparable metrics are available; internal scoring does not predict virality.

## Retention strategy
- Remove redundant setup where useful, but let important claims and shots breathe.
- Provide a reason to continue at every chapter/scene: new evidence, example, twist, development, visual demonstration or emotional progression.
- Use sound/music/cuts/holds to point at *meaning*, not to conceal missing information.
- Caption changes should match semantic units and real speech timing.
- Reactions and silence may be necessary for comedy, emotional interviews, aesthetic brand films and education.
- Deliver the promised answer or genuine payoff. Avoid fabricated urgency, unsupported exclusivity, withheld answers and deceptive clickbait.

## Evidence-based iteration
Compare versions varying one major lever: opening frame, spoken opening, claim order or preview of result. Track actual views/watch time/completion/shares/saves/clicks appropriate to objective and platform. Account for audience and exposure differences before drawing conclusions. Never invent a universal retention threshold.
