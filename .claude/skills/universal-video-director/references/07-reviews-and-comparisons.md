# Product review, comparison, unboxing and hands-on proof

## Audience contract
A review helps a buyer decide, not merely persuades. Say whether footage is hands-on, supplied by a brand, third-party, or a mock/demo. If no real use or evidence exists, create **review planning** or **specification overview**, not a first-person experience review. A comparison needs matching conditions and declared criteria.

## Recommended outline (flexible)
1. **Decision hook:** who should care and the main question (e.g., "รุ่นนี้คุ้มกว่าอีกรุ่นไหม?"). Lead with the product and real evidence, not an unrelated meme.
2. **Quick context:** price as of date (verified, currency included), use case, model/version, any affiliate/sponsor disclosure.
3. **Selection criteria:** 2–5 relevant dimensions such as ease of use, durability, maintenance, noise, design, value. Be consistent across compared options.
4. **Evidence:** close-up real product, unedited functional demonstration, source data, side-by-side visual with consistent scale or measured tests with method.
5. **Tradeoffs:** who should and should not buy; limitations and uncertainty. Genuine criticism builds credibility.
6. **Verdict:** distinguish measured findings, specifications and opinions. CTA may be "อ่านสเปก/ดูตอนทดสอบเต็ม" not forced purchase.

## Visual standards
- Accurate product color, markings, dimensions and packaging. Do not synthesize a non-existent feature in a supposedly real product demonstration.
- Label source: `actual demonstration`, `manufacturer footage`, `illustration`, `AI concept` or `simulation` when needed.
- Put measurement units, model names and test conditions on-screen. Avoid cuts that make a timed or measured test look stronger than it is.
- Real audio on operations where sound matters; record microphone/environment and avoid replacing noise with fabricated “test audio”.
- Use simple overlaid comparison tables; avoid massive captions hiding the product.

## Hook options
- "ของชิ้นนี้แก้ปัญหา X ได้จริงไหม?"
- "ผมเปรียบเทียบตาม 3 เกณฑ์นี้" **only if real comparison was done**.
- "ข้อเสียที่ควรรู้ก่อนซื้อ..." only with grounded cons.
- Lead with a visible feature or constraint, not just stock footage.

## QC
Check every price/claim/number in the claims ledger; verify supplier sources and test context. Never invent ratings, hands-on experience, customer testimonials or numerical measurements.
