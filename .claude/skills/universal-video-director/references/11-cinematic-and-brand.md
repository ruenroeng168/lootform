# Brand film, lifestyle, real estate, travel, fashion and event recap

## Strategy
These pieces sell a feeling, place, craft or experience. Pace can be slower than a social comedy edit. Start with the best visual or emotional stake, then sustain a coherent arc with appropriate color, sound and typography. Product/brand identity must remain accurate.

## Structure patterns
- **Brand story:** human situation → values in action → earned visual/emotional payoff → unobtrusive brand recall/CTA.
- **Real estate:** honest property context → flow/spatial layout → key rooms/functional proof → factual specifications → contact/visit CTA. Avoid misleading ultra-wide views, absent defects or digitally inserted amenities shown as real.
- **Travel:** true place ID, dates/conditions when relevant, recognizable visuals, useful context; tag generic illustrative shots.
- **Event recap:** establish setting, peak moments, faces/atmosphere (with permission), reactions, closure and credit.
- **Portfolio/craftsmanship:** show process and detail, demonstrate quality from real evidence, avoid inflated award/credential claims.

## Visual language
Choose and document contrast, palette, transitions, camera motion, lens feel, grain, type, logo timing, music and sound design. Keep motion consistent; cut to action or music beat when meaningful, not every beat. Use elegant negative space and slower holds for hero product/architecture shots. For before/after visuals, ensure honest comparability and label visualization concepts.

## Deliverables
Master in requested ratio/resolution, platform cutdowns with manual crop review, a cover/thumbnail, logo placement and caption/credit notes. Verify logos, brand names, locations, price and contact text before export.
