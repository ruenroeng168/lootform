# Hook strategy by goal, platform and evidence

A strong hook is a clear **promise of value or emotion** delivered in the video, not simply a loud first second. Use specific visible evidence where available and respect cultural context. Generate 3 different mechanisms, not 3 paraphrases.

| Video objective | Hook archetypes | Honest proof of fulfillment |
|---|---|---|
| Product review | meaningful buyer question; surprising verified limitation; show real test | unaltered demonstrations, declared criteria, tradeoffs |
| Sales | relevant pain + visible result; use-case; verified limited offer | accurate product shot, authorized specs/price/terms |
| Comedy | confrontation; absurd mismatch; expectation reversal | escalation + earned punchline, reactions |
| Education | direct answer; common mistake; compelling diagram | clear teaching sequence and correct example |
| Documentary | authentic moment; central unresolved question | sourced chronology, context and perspective |
| Interview/podcast | representative compelling answer or stakes | complete quote, not deceptive splicing |
| Brand/real estate | striking authentic visual; emotional mission; place transformation | true location/product shots and actual capabilities |
| Tutorial | final outcome + simple first step | demonstration from start to workable result |

## Five-dimension internal hook score (0–5 each)
1. **Clarity:** the audience recognizes topic and intended benefit.
2. **Relevance:** matches a real audience question, pain or aspiration.
3. **Curiosity/tension:** reason to continue, without ambiguity for its own sake.
4. **Credibility:** does not inflate evidence, numbers or urgency.
5. **Fulfillment:** body actually answers or pays off the opening.

Choose the best for the goal; a flashy comedy-style hook can score poorly for professional education. Do not assert that the highest internal score forecasts platform distribution. Test first-frame A/B when actual metrics become available. A lower-intensity hook is valid for documentary, premium brand or serious topics.

## Length expectations
No arbitrary universal timing. Social clips often benefit from rapid clarity; long-form can use a deliberate opening and chapter-level rehooks. Spoken timing uses voice playback, not text length guesses. Make sure opening graphics and captions don't hide the subject.
