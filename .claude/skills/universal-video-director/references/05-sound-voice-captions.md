# Audio, voice, music, captions and accessibility

## Adapt sound to format
- **Comedy:** contrasting performances and well-timed reactions; short SFX punctuate, don't overwhelm.
- **Review / ad:** natural voice, real product/test sound when relevant, credible demo audio, precise prices/terms.
- **Education:** intelligibility, pauses for complex ideas, terms/units spelled correctly, intentional silence between steps.
- **Interview/documentary:** preserve authentic voices, room tone and qualifiers; repair noise gently, avoid artificial emotional misrepresentation.
- **Cinematic/brand:** music and soundscape can lead; make dialogue intelligible when present.

## Voice production
Use user-provided voice when available; otherwise authorized TTS with speaker consistency and appropriate locale. No unconsented real-person cloning. Measure real audio files rather than computing duration from word counts. Confirm Thai accents/slang, loanwords, names, units and stress. If there is no voice track, **silent by design** is valid; do not invent one or fail a silent video for lacking speech.

## Subtitles
- For dialogue, time to actual recording via manual timing or verified speech alignment; timestamps derived from character counts are guesses.
- Thai captions: phrase-based segmentation, correct glyph/diacritic display, high contrast, max 1–2 lines where appropriate. Don't cut sentences in misleading ways.
- For interviews label speakers only when verified; for tutorials preserve exact code/numbers/units; for sales keep legal/offer copy accurate.
- Create editable SRT/VTT when requested; inspect actual output to ensure captions are on-screen and not under UI overlays.

## Sound mix
Prioritize audible speech where relevant. Duck music intentionally and fade edits. Avoid clipping and abrupt syllable cuts, inconsistent speaker levels or noisy SFX. Platform loudness targets are not universal; report measured results, not assumed compliance. All music/SFX rights must be logged.

## QA
Check full audio playback (if tools permit), opening/closing transitions, synchronization and music usage rights. If no playback is available, mark review as NOT CHECKED. For silent designs check that silent delivery was intentional and communicated.
