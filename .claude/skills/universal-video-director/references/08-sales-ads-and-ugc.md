# Direct-response ads, ecommerce listings, product sales and UGC

## Primary goal
Sell **without false claims**. Distinguish brand-generated material, paid creators, real testimonials, acted scripts and AI personas. The objective can be awareness, consideration, lead, add-to-cart or purchase; a strong CTA matches the funnel, platform and actual offer.

## Choose a persuasion structure
- **Problem → solution → demonstrated benefit → credible proof → truthful offer → CTA.** Best when the problem is clear and evidence exists.
- **Use-case demo → feature → user outcome → tradeoff → CTA.** Best for visible products.
- **Before / after:** only use genuine before/after from comparable conditions and with permission. Do not fabricate transformations or promise typicality without evidence.
- **Objection handling:** articulate a buyer concern and answer with verified specifications, return policy, warranty, availability or real demo.
- **UGC/story:** conversational language, lived detail only if genuine. If scripted/acted, avoid implying an unpaid real customer endorsement.

## Required sales brief fields
- Audience and purchase stage; product/category; accurate SKU/model and photo; verified benefit, limitation and differentiator.
- Actual price/promotion, currency, dates, stock, shipping regions, warranty, returns, terms and checkout destination; otherwise **leave claims blank or flagged `UNVERIFIED`**.
- Brand tone and compliance/legal constraints (especially cosmetics, medical claims, financing, income claims and regulated categories).
- Primary conversion goal and CTA, single key objection, mandatory disclosure.

## Hook families
- Show the product solving a pain point **on footage that proves it**.
- Clear use-case: "สำหรับคนที่..." (avoid exaggerated universality).
- Demonstrate a distinguishable feature with measured or manufacturer-backed terms.
- Offer hook only when the offer is verified and current.
- Story hook + offer works when the story is relevant, not clickbait.

## Edit choices
- Clean product visibility, close-ups and correct crops. Keep SKU, price, terms readable.
- On-screen cards: product name, one claim at a time, prices/terms when verified, single CTA.
- Avoid stock-looking fake unboxing, fake comment screenshots, fabricated review stars, artificial countdown urgency, or unproven "best selling" badges.
- Separate a 6/15/30/60-second version where supported, but don't mechanically speed up speech; prioritize clarity and correct terms.
- For multi-product listings use consistent neutral visual treatment and variant labels.

## Handoff
Include copy variants, thumbnail/cover, voiceover, storyboard, export formats, creative test hypothesis, proof/claims ledger and rights manifest. Conversion claims require actual analytics; do not invent CTR/CVR.
