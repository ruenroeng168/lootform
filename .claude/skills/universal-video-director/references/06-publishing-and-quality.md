# Publishing, creative operations, measurement and release quality

## One brief, several deliveries
Choose exports from the brief rather than assuming every video belongs to Shorts/TikTok:
- short vertical 9:16 with mobile-safe captions when requested;
- landscape 16:9 for tutorials, long-form YouTube or presentations;
- square 1:1 or 4:5 for feed/advertising when relevant;
- original edit, versioned cutdowns, thumbnail/cover, SRT/VTT and posting copy when actually requested.

## Release package (create only existing, meaningful files)
`brief.md`, `claims-ledger.csv` for factual/marketing clips, `asset-manifest.csv`, `script.md`, `storyboard.md`, optionally `story.json` and character/style bible, `exports/*`, `qa/report.md`, and tailored post captions and disclosure notes. Do not claim any MP4, SRT, picture or sound file exists unless verified.

## Universal scorecard (0–5, descriptive not predictive)
1. Audience relevance and main point clarity
2. Hook/promise fulfillment
3. Credibility/evidence or creative authenticity
4. Structure and pacing appropriate to the format
5. Visual/art direction and platform-safe composition
6. Audio, captioning and accessibility
7. Appropriate ending/CTA and brand integrity

No score compensates for material false claims, missing media, unlicensed assets or absent playback review.

## Post-publish learning
Only after actual metrics are provided: log platform, target audience, video goal, length, format, creative variant, watch-time metrics available, sharing/saving, clicks, leads, conversions if tracked, creator notes, and material differences across test cells. Avoid claiming causality from tiny or uncontrolled samples. For education assess knowledge/feedback; for reviews trust and saves; for direct response qualified conversions; for comedy shares/watch; for brand film qualified reach and sentiment; do not use a universal KPI.

## Approval gate
Fill `templates/release-checklist.md` as PASS / FAIL / NOT CHECKED / NOT APPLICABLE. For fact-sensitive reviews and ads, the claims ledger must have no unresolved material claims. For interviews, source footage context must be preserved. For generated video, actual audio/visual playback is required for a full APPROVED status, otherwise PROVISIONAL.

## Rights and responsible publication
Clarify commercial licenses, music/voice rights, sponsorship/affiliate disclosure, footage permissions, likeness permission and synthetic media disclosure where context/platform requires it. Never imply a paid promotion is an independent verified personal testimonial. Preserve original files and editable projects for corrections.
