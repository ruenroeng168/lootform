# Art Studio — illustration, composition, prompting and continuity

## Pick the right visual route
A. **User footage/photos:** retain true appearance, crop/grade/annotate appropriately; back up originals.
B. **Licensed stock/third-party:** verify terms, attribution and relevance; never imply footage depicts the actual product/location/test if it does not.
C. **Generated images:** use an authorized available image tool; record prompt/reference/seed when known and inspect actual results. No claim of generated art if only a prompt was written.
D. **Code drawings:** SVG, React, CSS and layered vector shapes for charts, title cards, diagrams, motion graphics, recurring character cutouts. Code-only artwork is not photoreal evidence.

## Universal style bible
Format and aspect, color palette, font and Thai coverage, typography hierarchy, spacing, captions/safe zones, icon/shape vocabulary, motion vocabulary, lighting/grade, logo rules, source authenticity, copyright provenance. Match style to viewer objective: factual review and education often need clean neutral graphics; comedy can use exaggerated character poses; brand films may be cinematic and restrained.

## Characters (only if format needs them)
Use `templates/character-bible.md`: identity, silhouette, wardrobe, colors, consistent facial features, expressions, voice style and image references. Maintain stable IDs across shots. Do not force recurring characters into product review, silent explainer or podcast highlight JSON.

## Image prompt recipe
`subject + required verifiable properties + viewpoint / composition + action / emotion + environment + light + style + safe area + negative constraints + rights / label requirements`

Example for **fictional comedic illustration**: "Stylized fictional civil engineer in an orange safety helmet, waist-up, annoyed expression, construction-site backdrop, clean flat vector animation style, 9:16, no logo, no embedded text, reserve subtitle area." Do not use an image prompt as evidence of real construction methods or product functionality.

## Layer and continuity rules
- Keep background, character/product, overlays, captions and brand marks separate when possible.
- Build legible compositions for the requested 9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 or custom ratio.
- Do not bake Thai captions into generated imagery; typeset captions in editing software with verified fonts.
- Review hands, shapes, labels, product geometry, real logo/packaging correctness, perspective, color, consistent framing and cross-shot continuity.
- Keep movement accessible: limit flashes, excessive shake and unreadably quick animations.

## Approval
Contact-sheet review, asset manifest/licensing, metadata honesty and correct product identity before rendering or publication. If the image tool is unavailable, deliver a professional SVG/diagram or detailed art plan—not a claim that an image was produced.
